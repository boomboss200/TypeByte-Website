Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

TypeByte ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what we do not do with it.

What We Collect

When you join our waitlist, we collect:

  • First name — so we can address you personally
  • Email address — to notify you when TypeByte launches
  • Occupation and experience level — to better understand our audience (optional)
  • Interested plan — to understand which tier resonates with you (optional)

What We Do Not Collect

TypeByte is built on a privacy-first principle:

  • We never store what you type. Text you type or rewrite in the keyboard goes directly to the AI model and nowhere else.
  • No message logs. We do not store, read, or retain any of your messages, rewrites, or voice recordings on our servers.
  • No keystroke logging. We have no access to what you type in any app.
  • Stats stay local. Your usage statistics live in on-device storage only.

How We Use Your Information

We use your email and name solely to:

  • Send you a notification when TypeByte is available for early access
  • Communicate important updates about the product

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Storage

Waitlist data is stored securely using Supabase, a hosted database provider. Your data is stored in accordance with their security and privacy standards.

AI Processing

When you use the TypeByte keyboard, rewrite requests are sent directly to OpenAI's GPT-4o Mini model. TypeByte does not intercept, log, or retain the content of these requests. Please refer to OpenAI's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle API requests.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your waitlist entry at any time by emailing us at colabwithcreatebyte@gmail.com. We will remove your data promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the waitlist or app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at colabwithcreatebyte@gmail.com.
